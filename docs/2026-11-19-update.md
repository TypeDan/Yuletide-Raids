## Native validation on 19 November

AoE IV 17.0.12303, native Content Editor builds with zero errors and warnings:

- Set Player 1 to 250 population and Player 2 to +20% economy and +50% military through the actual lobby controls. In the live match, native slot 1 was the human host with maximum population 250 and no bonuses; native slot 2 was the AI with maximum 200, economy multiplier 1.2 and military multiplier 1.5. Internal player iteration order was reversed, confirming the settings follow lobby slots. The ordinary resource HUD displayed 7/250 and the small Pop 250 line appeared directly above it.
- Santa plus all nine reindeer remained world-owned on every sampled tick. The full convoy travelled 799 engine distance units, completed nine of nine selected present drops, then despawned. The route crossed multiple waypoints. This removes the earlier player-owned convoy and its tracking advantage; pink ownership is unnecessary.
- The engine ignores the standard Cmd_Move wrapper for already-neutral groups. Queueing native movement orders and relinquishing ownership in the same simulation callback preserves movement throughout the visit. No later owner transfer occurs. Both cart and reindeer rendered and moved; close-up verification of each model's full animation cycle remains limited.
- The gift shop opens. Goods/Raids/Buffs tabs fit; the enemy-name button displays the native name and selects the correct enemy. An Age I armoured purchase deducted one token and spawned small units fighting at the selected AI base. Only current-age choices were shown.
- Festive Cheer purchased successfully in an earlier native test: one token deducted and the 30-second countdown appeared. Exact gathering throughput still relies on the automated modifier tests.
- The temporary diagnostic supplied tokens, accelerated one visit and revealed the map for observation. Those hooks and their overlay are removed from release source. Full map visibility in diagnostic screenshots is not normal gameplay.

All nine Yuletide and both shared MAGS test scripts passed after diagnostic removal. They cover all eight slots, caps, bonus separation, reward conservation, deposit clearance, all age tiers, neutral routes, present limits and buff expiry/refund/non-stacking behavior.
